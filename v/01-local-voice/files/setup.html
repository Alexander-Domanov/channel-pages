Setup rules

Four things decide whether this works or eats your whole evening.

  • Two environments, not one. One model needs the library at 5.3 or newer, the other is pinned to 4.57. Together they break. Keep each one in its own environment.
  • Read the license before you record anything. Both models here are Apache 2.0, which lets you monetize the output. Plenty of open models don't. License first, weights second.
  • The weights are a download. Plan for it. One model pulled its files in about four and a half minutes on a normal connection, the other was around four gigabytes.
  • Memory isn't the problem. Peak was 1.76 GB out of 8. You don't need a workstation, an 8 GB machine gets it done.

The exact install commands are in the video. These rules are the stuff the commands can't tell you.
