Voice sample rules

Record it once, use it everywhere. Whatever you hand the model is what it sounds like forever.

  • Length: 3–10 seconds is plenty. Longer doesn't help.
  • Quiet room, one take. The model copies the room along with the voice.
  • The accent comes from the sample, not the language. A Russian sample gives you Russian-accented Spanish. For Mexican Spanish, record a Spanish sample, about 8–9 seconds.
  • One sample per language. Two languages, two files, one recording session.
  • Speed carries over too. A slow, calm read comes back slow and calm. A fast one comes back rushed. Pick the pace you want on every future video on the day you record.
  • You can't describe a voice in words. The model takes a fixed list: gender, age, pitch, accent, whisper. Cloning from a sample is easier and sounds better.

Why the voice is worth the trouble

People subscribe to a voice the same way they subscribe to a face. Once it's yours, it stays yours across every video, and you never pay by the minute again.
