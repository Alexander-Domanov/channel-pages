How long you'll wait

I timed it instead of guessing. Both programs ran on the same laptop, on the same afternoon.

ProgramTwenty minutes of narration
First oneabout twenty minutes
Second oneabout an hour

So start with the first one. The wait is roughly the length of the video you're making, which means you can write the next script while it reads the current one. The laptop stayed responsive the whole time, I didn't have to leave it alone.

Nothing about this costs money, and there's nothing to top up. You can run it at midnight as many times as you want.

The quality switch

Both programs have a quality setting. Turning it all the way up made almost no difference to how it sounded, and made the wait four times longer. Leave it where it starts.

What this means for a real channel

A video with twenty minutes of talking is ready in about twenty minutes of waiting. Three videos a week is under an hour of computer time, and you can do it while you do something else.
