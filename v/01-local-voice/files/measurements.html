How long you'll wait

Timed end to end on one ordinary laptop, on one afternoon. No guessing.

ProgramTwenty minutes of narration
First oneabout twenty minutes
Second oneabout an hour

Start with the first one. The wait is roughly the length of the video you're making, so you can write the next script while it reads the current one. The laptop stays responsive the whole time, which means you don't have to sit and watch it.

Nothing here costs money and there's nothing to top up. Run it at midnight as many times as you want.

The quality switch

Both programs have a quality setting. Turning it all the way up made almost no difference to how it sounded, and made the wait four times longer. Leave it where it starts.

What this means for a channel

A video with twenty minutes of talking is ready after about twenty minutes of waiting. Three videos a week is under an hour of computer time, and you can spend that hour doing something else.
