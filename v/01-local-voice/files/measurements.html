Test your own computer

Same program, different chip, different answer. Two minutes of work gives you your own number, and it stays useful long after these numbers go stale.

  1. Take a piece of text that reads for twenty to thirty seconds.
  2. Time the run with your phone.
  3. Divide the waiting time by the length of the speech.

Twenty-two seconds of speech in twenty-six seconds gives 1.2. That's comfortable: a twenty-minute narration finishes in about twenty-five minutes. Around 3 is workable if you leave it running and do something else. Past 5, use the faster program or make shorter videos.

The version numbers in every guide move. The ratio is the part that stays.

The computer

  • Heaviest memory use: under two gigabytes out of eight. The laptop stays usable.
